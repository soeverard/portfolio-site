About

UI/UX DesCrafting Intuitive Experiences, Elevating Digital Innovation. Explore the Fusion of Art and Functionality in Every Pixel.

Hello! I'm Alexandra Bennett, a passionate UX designer driven by a love for creating memorable digital experiences. With a background in cognitive psychology, I bring a unique blend of analytical thinking and creative problem-solving to the world of design.

I earned my degree in Cognitive Psychology from Plymouth University, where I developed a deep understanding of human behavior and decision-making processes. This foundation plays a pivotal role in my approach to crafting user-centric designs.

My journey in UX design has been dynamic and fulfilling. I've had the opportunity to work with diverse teams and industries, honing my skills in creating interfaces that seamlessly merge form and function.
